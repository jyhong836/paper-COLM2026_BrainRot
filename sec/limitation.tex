\subsection*{Limitations}

As a first attempt at systematically examining the effects of continual pre-training on junk web text, our study is intentionally scoped to social media content from Twitter/X. While this domain offers a large volume of naturally ``engaging but semantically shallow'' text, it represents only one slice of the broader online ecosystem. Cross-platform (e.g., Reddit, YouTube comments, TikTok transcripts) and cross-lingual validation remain essential next steps for assessing the generalizability of our findings.
